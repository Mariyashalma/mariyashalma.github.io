Common questions

Do you work with clients in Europe?

Yes, most current work is for European clients. India is 3½ to 4½ hours ahead of central Europe, so European mornings overlap with Indian afternoons.

What happens to our data?

The system runs in your own Azure account, and no passwords or keys are stored in the code. An NDA is fine. Client documents never appear in public demos.

Is a small project fine?

Yes. One dashboard, one automated workflow or one data analysis is a normal place to start.
